### Confirmed Defects (Must Fix Before Migration)

1. **Client-Supplied `created_at` Spoofing on `INSERT` (`lab5_set_timestamps`, lines 9–10)**
* **Location:** `public.lab5_set_timestamps()` (`IF TG_OP = 'INSERT' THEN NEW.created_at := COALESCE(NEW.created_at, ...)`).
* **Defect:** Although `created_at` is omitted from `GRANT INSERT (...) TO authenticated` on `properties` and `sponsors`, any elevated or custom role with `INSERT` privileges (or future grant changes) can backdate or future-date `created_at` via `COALESCE`.
* **Fix:** Unconditionally overwrite `NEW.created_at := timezone('utc'::text, clock_timestamp());` on `INSERT`.


2. **Server-Side Approval Consistency Gap (`properties_approval_consistency`, `sponsors_approval_consistency`)**
* **Location:** `public.properties` (`CONSTRAINT properties_approval_consistency CHECK ((status = 'approved') = (approved_at IS NOT NULL))`) and `public.sponsors` (`sponsors_approval_consistency`).
* **Defect:** Neither constraint checks `approved_by`. A `service_role` moderation action can approve a row while leaving `approved_by IS NULL`, or set `approved_by` on a `draft`/`suspended` row without `approved_at`. Additionally, when transitioning from `'approved'` to `'suspended'`, `approved_at` and `approved_by` must be cleared (`NULL`) or the check constraint will raise `23514` (`status <> 'approved'` while `approved_at IS NOT NULL`).
* **Fix:** Document/enforce clearing `approved_at` and `approved_by` on suspension, or enforce `((status = 'approved') = (approved_at IS NOT NULL AND approved_by IS NOT NULL))` if server moderation always passes an admin/service profile UUID.


3. **Unintended `authenticated` Read Access to Sensitive/Internal Columns (`GRANT SELECT ON public.properties` / `public.sponsors`)**
* **Location:** Section 4 (`GRANT SELECT ON public.properties TO authenticated;` and `GRANT SELECT ON public.sponsors TO authenticated;`).
* **Defect:** Unlike `anon` (which has explicit column grants), `authenticated` is granted table-wide `SELECT`. Because `properties_public_read_approved` and `sponsors_public_read_approved_active` apply `TO anon, authenticated`, **any** logged-in user can read `owner_id`, `approved_at`, `approved_by`, and `updated_at` on *every* approved property and sponsor across tenants.
* **Fix:** Restrict `authenticated` to the same public column list as `anon` for cross-tenant reads, or route owner-only metadata (`approved_at`, `updated_at`) through an owner-scoped view/RPC.


4. **Missing `UPDATE` Column Grants & Policy on `property_sponsors` vs. Foreign Key Cascades**
* **Location:** `public.property_sponsors` (`REFERENCES public.properties(property_id) ON DELETE CASCADE` and `REFERENCES public.sponsors(sponsor_id) ON DELETE CASCADE`), Section 4 grants, and Section 5 policies.
* **Defect:** `property_sponsors` has `ON DELETE CASCADE` on both FKs, which is safe when a draft property or draft sponsor is deleted by its owner. However, if a `sponsor` owner deletes an *unapproved* sponsor, any junction rows vanish silently; conversely, once a sponsor is `approved`, the sponsor owner cannot delete it (`sponsors_owner_delete_unapproved`), preventing unilateral link breakage. The defect is that **a property owner cannot unlink an approved sponsor once the property becomes `approved**` (`property_sponsors_owner_delete_unapproved` checks `p.status IN ('draft', 'pending')`). If a sponsor later becomes `suspended` or `is_active = false`, the link remains in `property_sponsors` (hidden from public `SELECT` by RLS, but unremovable by the property owner).


5. **Role-Check Bypass Potential in `lab5_enforce_property_invariants` / `lab5_enforce_sponsor_invariants**`
* **Location:** `is_client_role boolean := (current_user IN ('anon', 'authenticated') OR jwt_role IN ('anon', 'authenticated'));`
* **Defect:** In PostgREST/Supabase, `service_role` requests execute as `authenticator` -> `SET LOCAL ROLE service_role` with `request.jwt.claim.role = 'service_role'`. However, if a `SECURITY DEFINER` function owned by `postgres` runs on behalf of an `authenticated` user, `current_user` becomes `postgres`, and it relies solely on `request.jwt.claim.role`. If `request.jwt.claim.role` was reset or unset in a background worker, `is_client_role` evaluates to `false`.
* **Fix:** Default-deny unless explicitly `service_role` or table owner (`is_privileged boolean := (current_user IN ('postgres', 'supabase_admin', 'service_role') AND COALESCE(jwt_role, 'service_role') = 'service_role');`).

### Design Ambiguities & Architectural Risks (Require Product Confirmation)

* **Anonymous vs. Authenticated Inquiries (`inquiries_public_insert_approved_property` & `GRANT INSERT ... TO anon, authenticated`):**
* `ai-studio-review.md` notes `design.md` specifies **authenticated-client insertion** for inquiries, whereas `ddl-proposal-attempt-02.txt` grants `INSERT` to both `anon` and `authenticated`. Allowing unauthenticated `anon` inserts exposes the endpoint to unthrottled spam/PII injection without a `requester_id` (`auth.uid()`) audit trail.




* **Owner Read Access to Inquiry PII (`inquiries_owner_read_own_property`):**
* Granting `SELECT` on `inquiries` to `authenticated` exposes `requester_email` and `message` directly to the property owner's client session. Confirm whether Lab 5 requires direct owner `SELECT` or `service_role`-mediated forwarding.




* **Primary Key Mutability (`property_id text PRIMARY KEY`, `sponsor_id text PRIMARY KEY`):**
* Using natural slugs (`text`) as primary keys without `ON UPDATE CASCADE` on `property_sponsors` and `inquiries` makes slug renames impossible after creation without manual multi-table migrations.

### Optional Hardening

* **Explicit `WITH CHECK` on `inquiries` Email/Header Injection:** Add `requester_name !~ '[\r\n]'` and `requester_email !~ '[\r\n]'` in `CHECK` constraints to prevent CRLF header injection if inquiry rows are forwarded via SMTP by a server worker.
* **Composite Index for Owner Inquiry Queries:** Replace `idx_inquiries_property` on `(property_id)` with `(property_id, created_at DESC)` to optimize chronological inbox queries per property.
* **Explicit `REVOKE ALL ON FUNCTION`:** Revoke `EXECUTE` on `lab5_set_timestamps()`, `lab5_enforce_property_invariants()`, and `lab5_enforce_sponsor_invariants()` from `PUBLIC`, `anon`, and `authenticated` (triggers execute via table ownership, so direct RPC invocation by clients is unnecessary).
* **`DEFAULT` on `owner_id`:** Add `DEFAULT auth.uid()` on `properties.owner_id` and `sponsors.owner_id` so PostgREST clients do not have to pass `owner_id` explicitly in payloads.
