# Value proposition — one-page simulated discovery draft

**Status:** INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION; team review pending. The source is a classroom
simulation, not customer research. Proposed owner: Eric, with all-team review.

## Problem and proposed value

Property professionals may need to keep listing facts accurate without repeating
work, while local sponsors may need to publish clear, time-bounded offers with
minimal effort. In the simulation, these groups have different freshness and
approval preferences; they must not be collapsed into a single generic publisher.

The proposed Neighborhood Listing Platform helps property publishers prepare
accurate, accessible listing information and gives sponsors a preview/review path
for visibly disclosed offers. A controlled publication/correction workflow would
separate a publisher's sign-off from platform approval. Privacy-conscious inquiry
and aggregate measurements are candidate directions, not proven user demand.

## Known facts versus assumptions

Known from the supplied assignment: there are two discovery stakeholder groups,
the course requires permission-based outreach or a labeled simulation, eight
stories and a planning board. Known from the repository: Lab 3 validates fictional
property/sponsor data and the midterm supplies services/bookings. Those are distinct
implemented slices; property/sponsor authoring and the workflow proposed here are
not claimed complete.

Assumptions: the fictional publishers represent useful candidate workflows; a
lightweight authoring/review process will be useful; proposed owners and effort
sizes are suitable. These require team review and, for real product claims,
stakeholder confirmation. No demographic traits, business statistics, market
size, legal compliance or conversion improvement is established.

## Candidate success measures and scope

Use a fictional acceptance walkthrough to check required-field validation,
review/visibility transitions, sponsorship labeling, offer end-date behavior and
keyboard-readable content. Candidate future product measures include aggregate
inquiries/clicks; baseline, definitions and collection limits need confirmation.
No numeric business target or measured improvement is invented.

The [Week 8 MVP](mvp.md) focuses on accurate drafts, separate approval, sponsor
disclosure/expiry and accessible presentation. Imports, automated property
reminders, sponsor appeals, POS redemption, fill-time analytics and localization
remain explicitly deferred or uncertain rather than presented as shared needs.
