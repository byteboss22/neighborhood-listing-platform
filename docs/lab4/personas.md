## P-B: local sponsor publisher

- Candidate goal: publish a clear offer with little effort (B01/B02) and assess

- Review: wants preview, suggests platform review and visible sponsorship labels

- Freshness: hard offer end date plus early pause (B05). Pre-expiry reminders and
  auto-renewal are not requested in the notes and remain open questions.

- Risk if false: sponsor review/measurement and publication workflows may vary.
