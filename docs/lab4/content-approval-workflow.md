## Roles and states

The following authority assignments are proposed policy. Who verifies facts,
who may hold each role and whether separate people are needed remain unresolved.

- Author: prepares/corrects owned content and attests accuracy/permission.
- Platform reviewer: checks required facts and records a decision/reason/revision.
- Authorized release/moderation operator: publishes only an approved revision and
  can unpublish inaccurate, unsafe or expired content with a recorded reason.
- Reader: sees only the eligible public projection, not review notes or contacts.

Proposed states: Draft → In review → Approved → Published. Changes requested or
Rejected records a reason and returns work to the author. Paused, Expired and
Unpublished stop public display. These are conceptual product states, not new
columns or changes to the midterm's prescribed service-status enum.

## Property path

1. A03 supports checking the address, photos showing the actual property, price
   and availability. Currency/period clarification is a proposed accuracy detail.
   A07 supports photo text alternatives and descriptions of accessibility features
   where present. A08 supports excluding public exact unit numbers/personal phone,
   keeping tenant details out and considering an email-hiding contact form.
2. A04 supports author sign-off. Requiring that sign-off to submit a revision for
   mandatory platform review is proposed D-02; it does not mean publication or
   that a second real person has approved it.
3. Proposed reviewer checklist: accuracy completeness and stated media permission.
   Extending image-permission checks from sponsor B03 to properties is a policy
   proposal, not an A03 request. Verification responsibility remains unresolved.
   Uncertain legal/advertising matters need qualified review; neither a model nor
   a checkbox establishes compliance, and no such review is claimed here.
4. Proposed release authority publishes an approved revision; public-facing edits
   require re-review under proposed D-03. Property freshness reconfirmation,
   reminder and expiry policy remains unresolved (A05).

## Sponsor path

1. B03 supports checking offer terms, dates, business hours and image-use
   permission; responsibility for checking claims remains unknown. Business
   identity, destination and precise time checks are proposed additions. Only
   fictional material is available here; no real business facts are verified.
2. B04 supports preview and clear sponsorship disclosure. Proposed wording is
   an explicit "Sponsored" label in preview and public content; "Local Partner"
   alone is not assumed sufficient. The exact wording is a design proposal.
   Preview does not establish sponsor sign-off or authorize publication.
3. Mandatory platform approval, a recorded decision and publication eligibility
   are proposed policy, beyond B04's suggestion of review. D-04 proposes UTC
   with a start-inclusive/end-exclusive window; timezone, display and exact
   end-date interpretation remain unresolved and require team confirmation.
4. B05 supports becoming non-public at the offer end date or early pause.
   Authority to perform release/pause actions is proposed policy. Reminders,
   auto-renewal and sponsor appeals are not claimed requested by the source.

## Corrections and unpublishing

Proposed policy: anyone may report a suspected error through a future defined
channel. A proposed authorized moderator may pause/unpublish questionable
content, privately record the reason/revision, notify the author through a
future agreed method and require corrected review before republishing. These
permissions, notifications and re-review rules are not existing functionality.
A04 supports property removal reasons and appeal; sponsor preferences remain
unknown. Eric owns any decision about appeal implementation or MVP scope.

A08 supports limiting public property contact details and excluding tenant data;
B08 supports totals without customer personal information or cross-visit tracking.
Proposed handling keeps internal review notes and personal contact details out of
public records, prompts and the repository. It does not settle what private data
must be collected, retained or deleted. Retention/access rules and notification
delivery remain unresolved; no legal compliance is established.
