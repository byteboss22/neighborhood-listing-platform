# Week 8 MVP — course-planning draft

This is a proposed planning scope for the assignment's Week 8 milestone, not a
backdated claim of delivery. Proposed owner: Eric; active-team confirmation needed.
The discovery is simulated. Estimates and priorities are provisional.

## Included outcome

A fictional property publisher can prepare accurate draft information and sign
off before platform review. A fictional sponsor can preview an offer, submit it
for review, and rely on visible sponsorship and end-date/pause exclusion. Readers
can access essential details as text and use the controls with a keyboard.

Included stories: US-01, US-02, US-03, US-04, US-05 and US-07 in
[the backlog](backlog.md). These are intended capabilities, not a statement that
the current Lab 3 static properties or midterm services already implement them.

## Explicit exclusions and uncertainty

- US-06 relay: deferred until delivery, data retention and anti-abuse are designed.
  In the MVP, do not expose private contact/tenant data as a substitute.
- US-08 automated property reminders/expiry: deferred. Do not silently apply
  sponsor expiry to properties; require an explicit future freshness decision.
- US-09 full appeal mechanism: deferred; at minimum document who can correct or
  unpublish and preserve a reason. Sponsor appeal needs remain unconfirmed.
- US-10 analytics: deferred. No resident identities/cross-visit tracking added
  merely to create a metric. POS redemptions/time-to-fill are not claimed covered.
- Bulk imports/syndication (A01), localization (B07), billing, geographic ranking,
  advanced campaigns and real outreach are outside this planning MVP.
